Privacy Policy

STPG Command Center app (Android, iOS and web) · Last updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains what the STPG Command Center app ("the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The app is operated by South Texas Proving Grounds ("STPG", "we") for its registered drivers and staff.

What we collect

DataWhyWhen
Account details: email, name, driver number, assigned vehicle, badge photoSign-in and showing who you are to STPG staff and your convoyWhen you sign in
Precise location, speed and headingMap, directions, weather, live convoy map, assistance/SOS requests, trip recordingWhile the app is open; during a recorded mission it can continue in the background
Live convoy positionLets other approved STPG drivers see where you areOnly while "Sharing" is on (you can turn it off on the Live Convoy screen)

Stays on your device only: recorded trips (GPX files), compass readings, voice-guidance settings, theme and tour progress. Spoken directions are generated on your device; the app does not record audio.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

Who we share it with

Only with the services the app needs to work:

  • Supabase — hosts accounts and app data (dispatches, missions, convoy positions, requests, hazards).
  • Google — Google Maps and route planning receive your location when you view the map or plan a route; Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications.
  • OpenStreetMap / Esri / OSRM — map tiles and routing when Google Maps is not used; they receive the map area or route points requested.
  • Open-Meteo — receives your approximate location to return the local weather.
  • Other approved STPG drivers and staff — see your name, driver number, convoy position (while sharing), and hazard pins. STPG staff see assistance requests.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety in an emergency.

How long we keep it

  • Live convoy position: only your latest position is stored; others see it for up to 30 minutes after your last update.
  • Hazard pins: removed from the map after 12 hours.
  • Assistance requests, dispatches and missions: kept as STPG operating records.
  • Account data: until your account is deleted.

Your choices

  • Turn off convoy sharing on the Live Convoy screen.
  • Deny or change location and notification permissions in your phone's settings (some features will stop working).
  • Delete recorded trips in Trip History.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it (below).

Delete your account

To delete your STPG Command Center account and its data, contact STPG through drivestpg.com from the email address on your account and ask for account deletion. We delete your profile, badge photo, convoy position, hazard pins and notification tokens within 30 days. Assistance requests may be kept in anonymized form as safety records.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited by account: only approved STPG drivers can see convoy data, and only STPG staff can see assistance requests and orders. On phones, sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.

Children

The app is for licensed drivers and STPG staff. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact STPG through drivestpg.com.
